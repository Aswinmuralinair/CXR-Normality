About the selected rule

"Normal" in this project means none of seven findings in the radiologist's report: atelectasis, cardiomegaly, consolidation, edema, effusion, pneumonia and pneumothorax. The lines were chosen on the NIH ChestX-ray14 dataset and do not carry over to another hospital's X-rays.
"Normal" here means none of seven findings: atelectasis, cardiomegaly, consolidation, edema, effusion, pneumonia and pneumothorax. A lung mass counts as normal. Labels come from report text.
